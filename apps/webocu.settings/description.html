WebOCU Settings is an overlay settings panel for webOS TV, driven by the remote. The video keeps playing under a transparent window while you change the options that the stock menus bury several levels deep.

  • Screen: energy saving level, and turning the screen off while the sound keeps playing.
  • Sound: choose the sound output, connect a paired Bluetooth audio device.
  • Power: restart or turn off the TV, each behind a confirmation.
  • System: operating system, device, network and storage information.
  • The panel's own look: themes, accent colour, background pictures, width, side, entrance effect, clock and date, twelve languages.

Root is optional. Without it the panel still works and some rows say that they need root; on a rooted TV the helper service gets elevated privileges, and the panel can start by itself after the TV boots.

Source and issues: https://github.com/webocu/webocu-settings
