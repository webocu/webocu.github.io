WebOCU Dock is an overlay app launcher for webOS TV: a small dock at the edge of the picture, driven by the remote or the Magic Remote pointer. It is drawn over whatever is on the screen without stopping video playback, so it can be called up at any moment.

  • A grid of your installed apps (1 to 7 columns, 3 to 7 rows) and the TV inputs.
  • Up to four quick buttons: Home, Settings, LG Store, Browser, Media Player, Eco mode, Sleep timer, Power Off, Restart, Screen Off, or a submenu.
  • Clock and date, dark, light and high-contrast themes, accent colours, background pictures, transparency.
  • Twelve languages.

Root is optional. Without it the dock works as is; on a rooted TV it can also get privileges for its helper service, stay alive in the background and open on the HOME button.
